Neighborhoods We Serve

Which Pierce County Neighborhood Fits Your Family's Lifestyle?

From Tacoma's urban waterfront to Gig Harbor's charming harbor town, Becca serves families across every corner of Pierce County. Explore our area guides to find the community that fits your life.

Tacoma, Ruston, and University Place

Washington's vibrant third-largest city and its neighboring communities offer urban energy, waterfront charm, and top-rated schools. From Stadium District's historic homes to University Place's family neighborhoods and Ruston's stunning Point Defiance waterfront, the greater Tacoma area has something for everyone.

Price Range: $300K - $750K+

Explore Tacoma Area

Bonney Lake, Lake Tapps, Edgewood, and Eatonville

East Pierce County offers everything from lakefront living at Lake Tapps to small-town charm in Eatonville near Mount Rainier. Bonney Lake and Edgewood deliver excellent schools, family-friendly parks, and stunning mountain views in a quieter setting.

Price Range: $250K - $600K+

Explore East Pierce County

Puyallup and Frederickson

Home to the famous Washington State Fair and the top-rated Puyallup School District, the Puyallup area offers charming downtown shops, South Hill's family neighborhoods, and Frederickson's growing community with competitive pricing.

Price Range: $350K - $600K+

Explore Puyallup Area

Gig Harbor and the Peninsula

One of Pierce County's most desirable communities, combining a charming waterfront downtown with the Peninsula School District (one of the best in the state). From luxury waterfront estates to family-friendly neighborhoods in Purdy and the Key Peninsula.

Price Range: $400K - $1.2M+

Explore Gig Harbor

Milton and Edgewood

Two charming small cities with tight-knit communities and convenient locations. Milton straddles the Pierce/King County border with easy commute access, while Edgewood offers larger lots, rural character, and Puyallup School District access.

Price Range: $400K - $600K+

Explore Milton & Edgewood

Eatonville and Graham

Rural charm, affordable acreage, and Pierce County's gateway to Mount Rainier. Graham offers growing subdivisions and equestrian properties, while Eatonville delivers genuine small-town living near Northwest Trek Wildlife Park.

Price Range: $250K - $500K+

Explore Eatonville & Graham

Common Questions About Buying and Selling Homes in Pierce County, WA

Commission is typically paid by the seller out of sale proceeds, so you don't pay me directly when buying. The standard in our market is 5-6% split between buyer and seller agents. When selling, we can discuss the commission structure that works best for your situation. There are no hidden fees or surprise costs.

Pierce County's market is dynamic and varies by neighborhood. Right now, we're seeing steady demand in family-friendly areas like Bonney Lake and strong interest in Tacoma's walkable neighborhoods. Pricing has stabilized after the rapid growth years, creating good opportunities for both buyers and sellers who understand the current conditions. I can provide you with detailed market analysis for your specific neighborhood and price point.

Absolutely. A home inspection protects you by identifying any issues before you commit. An appraisal ensures the property value supports your offer and protects your lender. Both are standard practice and I'll recommend trusted professionals who will give you honest, detailed reports. These typically cost $300-600 each and are well worth the investment.

For buyers, finding the right home typically takes 2-8 weeks, then closing takes 30-45 days after an offer is accepted. For sellers, listing to closing usually takes 30-60 days, though market conditions and your timeline matter. I'll set realistic expectations upfront and work to keep everything on track. Rush closing is possible in some cases.
